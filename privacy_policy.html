Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14/08/2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Scholarships & Internships ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

2. Information We Collect

We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to external servers. All information generated by your use of the app is stored locally on your device, including:

  • Liked or saved opportunities
  • Application tracking data
  • App preferences and theme settings
  • Subscription status

This data never leaves your device and is not accessible by us or any third party.

3. App Store Purchases

The app offers optional premium subscriptions (monthly and annual) via Apple's In-App Purchase system. When you make a purchase:

  • Payment is processed entirely by Apple through your Apple ID
  • We do not receive, store, or have access to your payment information
  • Subscription status is validated on-device using Apple's StoreKit 2 receipt validation
  • Subscriptions auto-renew unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period
  • You can manage or cancel your subscription in your Apple ID account settings

4. Third-Party Content

The app displays content sourced from RSS feeds. This content is aggregated from publicly available scholarship, internship, and competition listings. We do not modify the content of these feeds. When you tap on an opportunity, you may be directed to external websites that have their own privacy policies.

5. Analytics and Tracking

We do not use any analytics, crash reporting, or tracking SDKs. No data is sent to third-party analytics services. Your usage of the app is completely private.

6. Data Security

Since all data is stored locally on your device, it is protected by your device's built-in security features. If you delete the app, all locally stored data is permanently removed.

7. Children's Privacy

The app is suitable for all ages. We do not collect any personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
