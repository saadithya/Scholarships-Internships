Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15/08/2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Scholarships & Internships ("we," "our," or "us"). We operate the Scholarships & Internships mobile application and the website that publishes and provides information about scholarships, internships, competitions, grants, research opportunities, and other educational and professional opportunities.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use our mobile application. We are committed to protecting your privacy and minimizing the collection of personal information.

2. Information We Collect

The app does not require you to create an account or provide personal information in order to browse opportunities.

We do not intentionally collect, store, or transmit personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, home address, or account credentials through the app.

Information associated with your use of the app may be stored locally on your device, including:

  • Liked or saved opportunities
  • Application tracking information
  • App preferences and theme settings
  • Other locally stored app settings

This locally stored information is not transmitted to us by the app.

3. Opportunity Content and RSS Feeds

The Scholarships & Internships app displays opportunity information published through our own website. The app retrieves selected content from our website using RSS feeds and related web-based content services.

We research and publish information about opportunities available around the world, including scholarships, internships, competitions, grants, research opportunities, and other educational or professional opportunities.

The information published on our website may be based on publicly available information provided by universities, educational institutions, companies, organizations, government bodies, competition organizers, and other third-party sources.

We do not claim ownership of third-party opportunity information where such information belongs to the original publisher or provider. Where appropriate, users may be directed to the original source or external website for complete information and application details.

4. External Websites and Opportunities

The app may contain links to our website as well as links to external websites operated by universities, companies, organizations, scholarship providers, internship providers, competition organizers, and other third parties.

When you follow a link to an external website, you are leaving the Scholarships & Internships app. Those websites operate independently and may collect information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

We are not responsible for the privacy practices, content, security, or data collection practices of external websites.

We recommend reviewing the privacy policy and terms of any external website before submitting personal information or applying for an opportunity.

5. App Store Purchases

If premium subscriptions or other in-app purchases are offered in the iOS version of the application, payments are processed through Apple's In-App Purchase system.

When a purchase is made:

  • Payment is processed by Apple through the user's Apple ID.
  • We do not receive or store the user's credit card or other payment credentials.
  • Subscription and purchase information is handled through Apple's StoreKit services and the applicable purchase infrastructure.
  • Users can manage or cancel subscriptions through their Apple ID account settings, subject to Apple's applicable subscription terms.

6. Google Play

The Android version of the application is distributed through Google Play. Google may process information associated with the installation, distribution, security, and operation of applications through Google Play according to Google's own policies.

We do not receive your Google account password or payment credentials through the app.

7. Analytics and Tracking

We do not intentionally use the app to collect personal browsing profiles or track individual users across other applications or websites.

We do not sell personal information or use personal information collected through the application for targeted advertising.

8. Data Security

Information stored locally by the application is protected by the security mechanisms of your device and operating system.

If you uninstall the application, locally stored application data may be removed by the operating system.

9. Children's Privacy

The application provides information about educational and professional opportunities and does not require users to provide personal information to browse its content.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the application.

10. Opportunity Information and Accuracy

We make reasonable efforts to discover and publish useful opportunity information from publicly available sources. However, opportunity details such as eligibility requirements, deadlines, fees, availability, application procedures, and other conditions may change after publication.

Users should always verify the latest information with the original opportunity provider before submitting an application, paying a fee, or providing personal information.

The Scholarships & Internships app is an information and discovery service and does not guarantee acceptance, funding, employment, admission, or participation in any opportunity.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the application, website, services, or applicable requirements.

Any changes will be published on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
